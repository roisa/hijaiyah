Baby Mo Hijaiyah helps children learn to read the Qur'an, from the very first letter to short surahs — by playing with Baby Mo. The lessons follow the Iqra order, books 1–6 (sounds first, no spelling out, small steps), with the good habits of the Ummi method: talaqqi (Mo reads, your child copies), a little daily review, and a reading check with a parent before moving on. 7 BOOKS, 77 LEVELS • Book 1 · Fathah Island — all 28 letters with fathah • Book 2 · Joining Forest — joined letters and the long vowel • Book 3 · Kasrah-Dhammah Mountain — i and u sounds, mad • Book 4 · Tanwin & Sukun Sea — an in un, sukun, qalqalah • Book 5 · Tasydid Town — shaddah, ghunnah, al-, the word Allah, stopping • Book 6 · Tajwid Palace — nun and mim rules and mad, inside real verses • Al-Qur'an · Al-Fatihah and 14 surahs of Juz 'Amma, then a graduation certificate A GAME IN EVERY STEP Trace letters, pop balloons, ride the reading train, help Mo cross the bridge, match cards, build words and verses, sort the tajwid rules — all with clear Arabic audio. REWARDS THAT KEEP THEM GOING Stars in every level, a sticker book, combos, an island map that opens step by step, daily missions, streaks, and book tests with 3 hearts. Anything missed comes back gently at the end of the level. FOR PARENTS • A progress report for every book and a reading check in every level • English and Bahasa Indonesia, light and dark mode • No ads, no account, no tracking — progress stays on the device • Parent area behind a parental gate • Works offline The Qur'an text follows the Madinah Mushaf spelling.

Notes for App Review

Notes · App Review Information → Notes
Baby Mo Hijaiyah is a free app that teaches children (about 4–7 years old) to read Arabic letters up to short surahs of the Qur'an, as a game. There are no accounts, ads, in-app purchases, chat or social features. • No login: open the app, choose Bahasa Indonesia or English, optionally type the child's first name (stored only on the device), and play. • To see later levels quickly: Parents tab (answer the grown-up sum) → Settings → "Unlock all levels". • Microphone (optional): only for the "Record me" button, so the child can hear their own reading. The recording is kept in memory, never saved or uploaded. If permission is denied, everything else works. • Arabic audio plays recordings bundled with the app, with the device's speech voice as a fallback. The Qur'an recitation for the surah levels is bundled too. • No analytics or third-party SDKs; the app makes no network requests. The parent area is behind a parental gate (a maths question for grown-ups). • Works fully offline. Privacy policy: https://babymo.id/privacy/ · Support: https://babymo.id/support/
