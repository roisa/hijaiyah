Reel editor

Pick a reel, change the words, poses or timing below, press Play to preview. When you like it, Download the reel and send it to whoever renders the videos (node tools/reels/render.js my-reel.json), or paste it into kit/reels/reels.js to keep it.

How a reel works. steps is a list of [seconds, action, …]. Common edits: change a "title" or "say" text (both languages), change a pose in "mo" (pose-41 yes yeyy pose-02 wow pose-37 ok idea thank-you pose-12 pose-08 pose-20 alright pose-05 run pose-26 pose-33 pose-36 mo2-hug-quran abi-wave umi-welcome) or how Mo moves (in-left in-right jump wave spin pop out), change theme (mint sun sky pink grape gold night), or move a step's time. Game actions: tap ("answer", "wrong", "speaker", "start", "next"), trace (letter key: alif ba ta tsa jim hha kho dal dzal ro zai sin syin shod dhod tho zho ain ghin fa qof kaf lam mim nun wau ha ya), pop, train, bridge, build, finish. Effects: burst, confetti, letters, big, cta. Give a new reel a new id. The full list is at the top of kit/reels/engine.js.
